FAQ

Before you write

Quick answers for the questions we hear most often.

Where can I buy a specific flavor?

Availability varies by retailer and region. Use the Find a Store page for nearby options, and include your city if you still need help.

How do I report a packaging or product issue?

Choose Product Support and include the flavor, lot code if available, purchase location, and what you experienced. Photos help when something looks off.

Can teams or events request partnership support?

Yes — select Partnerships and share the event or team details, dates, audience size, and the kind of activation you have in mind.

Who should media contact?

Select Media and include your outlet, deadline, and the story angle. We’ll route campaign and press requests to the right contact.
